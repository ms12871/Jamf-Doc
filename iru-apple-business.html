Iru Endpoint / Apple Business

From purchase to first login.

A source-linked setup path for connecting Iru to Apple Business and enrolling organization-owned Macs, iPhones, and iPads with Automated Device Enrollment.

IRUformerly Kandji·current guide reviewed Sep 2026
Product names: Iru is the current name for Kandji. Apple’s current guide calls the business service Apple Business; many existing admin guides and tenants still say Apple Business Manager (ABM). Apple School Manager remains a separate education portal. Menu labels may change, so use Iru’s live documentation alongside this overview.
00

Before you start

Apple Business is the organization and enrollment portal; Iru Endpoint is the MDM that applies settings, apps, and security controls. Plan these prerequisites first:

  • An active Apple Business organization, with an administrator able to manage device management services and devices.
  • An Iru Endpoint tenant and an administrator account. Iru’s public Getting Started guide notes that trial tenants have a 10-device limit.
  • Organization-controlled Apple Account access for creating and renewing the APNs certificate. Keep the account and renewal owner documented.
  • Devices already assigned to the organization by Apple or an authorized reseller. For eligible existing devices, review Apple Configurator options.
  • A small test group, wireless/network access during Setup Assistant, and a decision about identity, apps, security controls, and which setup screens users should see.
01

Configure APNs in Iru

Apple Push Notification service (APNs) lets the MDM signal managed devices to check in. It is a separate setup from the Apple Business ADE server token.

  1. Open Iru Endpoint integrations.

    In Iru, open the Account Menu → Integrations → Apple → APNs setup.

  2. Create a new certificate for Iru.

    Follow Iru’s certificate wizard using an organization-controlled Apple Account. Iru’s current instructions say to create a new APNs certificate specifically for Iru Endpoint rather than reuse an existing MDM certificate.

  3. Record the owner and expiry date.

    APNs certificates require annual renewal. Retain access to the Apple Account used; renew in Iru before expiry and follow the same account and vendor workflow.

  4. Check network access.

    Allow required Apple device-management and APNs traffic. Apple documents client APNs traffic on TCP 5223, with TCP 443 fallback, and MDM-provider APNs traffic on TCP 443 or 2197. Confirm Iru’s current network requirements for your tenant.

02

Connect Apple Business to Iru ADE

This is the key token exchange. Start the setup in Iru; it provides a PEM public key. Apple Business uses that key to create an MDM service and returns a signed service token as a .p7m file.

  1. Start the ADE wizard in Iru.

    Open Account Menu → Integrations → Apple → Automated Device Enrollment → Set up. Save the PEM public key when Iru provides it.

  2. Add an MDM service in Apple Business.

    Sign in to business.apple.com with an account that can manage device services. Open Devices → Management → Add device management service. Give it a clear name such as “Iru Endpoint.”

  3. Upload Iru’s PEM file.

    Upload the PEM from the Iru wizard and continue. If Apple offers a “release devices” permission, grant it only if your operational policy requires Iru to release organization devices.

  4. Download Apple’s service token.

    Download the MDM service token from Apple Business. Keep the .p7m file secure and recognizable; it authorizes the link between this Apple Business MDM server and Iru.

  5. Return to Iru and upload the token.

    Complete the Iru wizard by uploading the Apple-signed .p7m token. Finish the setup and confirm the integration status in Iru.

  6. Keep renewal paired.

    When renewing, use Iru’s ADE renewal workflow and update the token in the connected tenant. Do not replace a token in Apple Business and leave Iru holding the old token.

Keep the files distinct: the Iru-provided PEM is uploaded to Apple Business; the Apple-downloaded .p7m token is uploaded to Iru. Neither is the APNs certificate or the Apps and Books content token.
03

Assign devices to Iru

  1. Confirm the serial numbers exist in Apple Business.

    New purchases from Apple or participating authorized resellers are typically added through the reseller assignment process. If a device is missing, ask the reseller to assign it; Apple Configurator may be an option for eligible devices.

  2. Assign the MDM server.

    In Apple Business, select the devices, choose Assign to Mobile Device Management (MDM) Server, select Iru Endpoint, and confirm. Default server assignment rules can reduce manual assignment for future purchases.

  3. Sync and select a Blueprint in Iru.

    In Iru Endpoint, open Enrollment → Automated Device Enrollment. Fetch devices if needed, verify they appear as Awaiting Enrollment, then assign the intended Blueprint or configure Blueprint Routing.

  4. Verify ADE settings.

    Check the ADE Library Item/profile associated with the Blueprint: authentication, Setup Assistant panes, enrollment behavior, and any required security bootstrap settings. Use only options supported for your device and OS version.

Devices assigned before activation can enroll during Setup Assistant. Apple documents that ADE is intended for organization-owned devices and can prevent the user from removing management when configured accordingly.

04

Connect Apps and Books

Apps and Books (formerly VPP) is a separate Apple integration and token. Use it to buy or acquire licenses and assign apps to users or devices from Iru.

  1. Finish APNs first.

    Iru’s setup sequence requires APNs before Apps and Books.

  2. Choose the right Apple Business organization or organizational unit.

    Use a dedicated token for the Iru tenant. Iru warns against sharing one Apps and Books token across multiple MDM servers.

  3. Start Apps and Books setup in Iru.

    Account Menu → Integrations → Apple → Apps and Books. Follow the connection flow to sign in to Apple Business and authorize the integration.

  4. Acquire licenses and assign apps.

    In Apple Business, acquire the needed app licenses. In Iru, add the apps to the Library and assign them through the intended Blueprint. Test installation and license behavior on a device.

05

Build a Blueprint before rollout

Blueprints define which Library Items apply to devices. Start small; separate baseline controls from department-specific apps and settings.

  1. Create a test Blueprint.

    In Iru Endpoint, open Blueprints → Add Blueprint. Start from scratch or a template, then name it for its role and scope.

  2. Add Library Items to its Assignment Map.

    Include only the baseline settings and essential apps needed for your test. Use conditional logic or Blueprint Routing where you need different configurations by user, device, or group.

  3. Review ADE Library Item settings.

    Configure user authentication and Setup Assistant behavior for enrollment. Avoid skipping panes users still need for accessibility, account setup, or organization policy.

  4. Set enrollment authentication deliberately.

    Choose whether the Mac should be assigned to a user and whether SSO/authentication is required. Confirm this matches the account-creation and identity workflow you actually support.

06

Test the first-run experience

Test a clean or properly erased device that has been assigned in Apple Business. Keep one known-good pilot device and document the expected setup screens and completion criteria before expanding to a larger group.

07

Renewal and troubleshooting

APNs

Renew annually through Iru’s integration workflow using the same organization-controlled Apple Account. Treat expiry as an incident risk; set a calendar reminder as well as relying on email notifications.

ADE token

Renew from Iru’s Apple integration. Confirm the new token is accepted and devices can sync after renewal. Follow Iru’s current process if the tenant has multiple ADE tokens.

Apps and Books

Keep the Iru content token current and associated with the intended organization/unit. Check license availability and app assignment if an app does not install.

New devices missing

Check reseller assignment and serial-number visibility in Apple Business. For eligible existing hardware, use Apple’s Configurator guidance; enrollment method and provisional-period behavior vary by device type.

Device is not appearing as Awaiting Enrollment

Confirm the serial number is in Apple Business, the device is assigned to Iru Endpoint (not just present in the portal), the ADE token is valid, and Iru has fetched devices. Check the Iru enrollment activity and token status.

Device starts setup but does not enroll

Verify the device was assigned before activation, APNs and network connectivity work, the Iru enrollment profile/ADE Library Item is assigned through the Blueprint, and the device can reach the required Apple and Iru services. Use Apple’s network requirements and Iru’s troubleshooting docs.

Blueprint or app did not apply

Check the enrollment-to-Blueprint assignment, Assignment Map conditions, app license availability, and Iru device activity. Test the exact same Blueprint and app assignment with one pilot device before broad rollout.
